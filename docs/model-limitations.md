# Model Limitations & Quantitative Disclaimers

## 1. Machine Learning in Non-Stationary Markets

Cryptocurrency markets are fundamentally non-stationary and governed by reflexive, adversarial dynamics:
1. **Regime Shifts & Macro Volatility**:
   - Macroeconomic news, CPI releases, regulatory announcements, and sudden liquidity shocks can violate historical statistical patterns.
   - Neural network weights trained on historical periods may exhibit elevated error when entering unseen volatility regimes.
2. **Autoregressive Compounding**:
   - In multi-step autoregressive generation ($h \ge 18$ candles), errors in earlier predicted steps can propagate and compound into wider uncertainty corridors.
   - Long horizons ($5d$) inherently carry wider prediction bounds than short horizons ($1d$).
3. **Liquidity & Spread Realities**:
   - Spot market prices displayed on public feeds do not guarantee fills for institutional block orders without market impact.
   - The paper research journal includes standardized 5 bps maker/taker fees and 5 bps slippage, but during extreme volatility spikes, realized execution slippage may be significantly wider.
